06 / BOUNDARIES THAT MATTER
Real experiences.
Responsible promises.
Business communication support is separate from bookings, passenger records, travel documentation and safety decisions.


Can I make a booking or check live travel availability here?+
No. The site is a business-services demonstration, not a booking engine. Availability, prices, reservations, cancellations, travel restrictions and operator terms must be verified through the relevant provider's official channels.


Can I provide passport details or booking references in this brief?+
No. The fixed-choice brief is for organisation-level needs only. Never add passports, travel documents, personal itineraries, payment-card details, passenger records or booking references to an unverified enquiry channel.


Can travel reservation or guest systems be integrated later?+
Any reservation, CRM or guest-record integration requires separate technical discovery, written scope, provider authorisation, privacy and security assessment, and applicable sector review. None is connected by this demonstration.
