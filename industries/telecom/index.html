06 / BOUNDARIES THAT MATTER
Clear expectations.
Better connections.
Business process support is separate from subscriber records, network operations and regulated telecom responsibilities.


Can Taamerat360 activate connections or resolve network faults?+
No. This page does not perform SIM registration, account verification, line activation, network engineering, outage resolution, billing changes or regulated telecom services.


Can I enter subscriber account or technical fault information?+
No. Do not provide phone numbers, subscriber IDs, identity documents, passwords, payment-card details, customer records, device identifiers or incident logs. Use the provider's official secure support channels.


Can Taamerat360 integrate with telecom operational systems later?+
Potential work would first require a separate technical assessment, authorised access, written security and privacy review, sector compliance approval and agreed scope. This demo has no billing, CRM, subscriber, provisioning or network integrations.
